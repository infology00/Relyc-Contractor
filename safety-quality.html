Safety

Safety is the first measure of a well-run jobsite.

Relyc plans every project around it — protecting our people, the client's property, and everyone on and around the site.

A history of zero accidents, injuries, or fault losses is not a slogan; it is the output of a repeatable program that starts at prequalification and runs through the last punch item.

  • Safety commitment & program — a written standard every crew and sub works to.
  • Site-specific safety planning — hazards identified and mitigated per site, before mobilization.
  • OSHA-aligned compliance & training — current certification tracked for every worker on site.
  • Daily toolbox talks / huddles — the day's hazards reviewed before work starts.
  • Subcontractor safety prequalification — safety record screened before a sub is eligible to bid.
  • Incident reporting & accountability — reported, investigated and closed out with corrective action.

Quality

Quality is controlled, not hoped for.

We manage materials, submittals, and workmanship to a defined standard from first inspection to final punch.

QA/QC program

A written quality plan per project, with hold points and sign-offs assigned to named people.

Inspections & mockups

Mockups approved before production work begins, so the standard is agreed rather than argued.

Material & submittal control

Every submittal reviewed against spec before release; substitutions documented, never assumed.

Punch list & closeout standards

Punch worked to completion before handover, with as-builts, warranties and O&M delivered.

What a Relyc jobsite looks like on day one.

Before the first delivery arrives, the site plan, the hazard analysis, the trade sequence and the quality hold points already exist on paper — and every sub has signed on to them.
