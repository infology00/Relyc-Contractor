How We Work

Single-source accountability, in practice.

We operate on single-source accountability: one contractor responsible for the entire project. Relyc self-performs general labor and supervision and coordinates a network of vetted specialty subcontractors — keeping quality, schedule, and communication under one roof.

01

We self-perform the core

General labor and site supervision stay in-house. That is where schedule slips and quality problems usually start, so that is what we refuse to hand off.

02

We vet every trade

Specialty subcontractors are prequalified on licensing, insurance, bonding capacity and safety record before they are eligible to bid, let alone mobilize.

03

You get one contact

One point of accountability from first call to warranty callback. No triangulating between trades to find out why a date moved.

Safety Record

Zero accidents.

Zero fault losses.

A history of zero accidents, injuries, or fault losses across our projects.

Safety is the first measure of a well-run jobsite, and it is inseparable from schedule and quality. Every Relyc project is planned around site-specific hazards, OSHA-aligned training and daily huddles — and every subcontractor is prequalified on safety before award.
