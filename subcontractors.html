For Trade Partners

Register your trade with Relyc.

We build our bid lists from a vetted subcontractor database. Register once, and your trade is considered as projects in your scope come up.

How Prequalification Works

01 · Register Submit your company, trades, licensing and coverage Along with your capability statement and current COI.
02 · Review We verify licensing, insurance, bonding and safety record Safety history is screened before a sub is eligible to bid.
03 · Bid list Approved trades are invited to bid in scope You will hear from us when a project matches your trade and capacity.
04 · Award Contract, insurance and bonding verified at award Then you work to our schedule, submittal process and punch standard.
